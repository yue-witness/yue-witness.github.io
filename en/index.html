By Eye

目测

My eyes are a ruler! One glance: how close can you guess?

  • Out now
  • Estimation puzzle
  • Android · Mini game
  • Multilingual
Get it on Google Play TapTap (China)

"That steak looks like half a pound." "Those two lines are definitely the same length!" Everyone says "looks about right", but is it really?

By Eye is a visual intuition puzzle game. No formulas, no calculations: take a glance, dial in your estimate and watch the reveal. Will it be a pinpoint triumph or a hilarious reality check?

Numbers are regenerated on every retry, so there is nothing to memorize.

Highlights

  • Six kinds of puzzles: proportions, quantities, moving objects, hands-on tasks, optical illusions, physics and geometry.
  • Day vs. night: take your time by day; at night the puzzle flashes once, then goes dark.
  • The reveal: 0.2% off or 28% off, you see it instantly.
  • Endless mode: unlocked after the day levels, with leaderboards.

Forge Simulator

每日强化挑战

Spend a fixed daily budget, weigh the risk, push your gear to +10.

  • Coming soon
  • Casual strategy
  • WeChat · Douyin mini games · TapTap
  • No in-app purchases
TapTap page (China)

Forge Simulator is a portrait-mode casual game. Each day you receive a limited stack of enhancement stones and split them across five slots (weapon, helm, armor, gloves and boots) to push each piece from +0 toward +10.

Every level's success rate, cost and score are shown up front. Low-level failures keep your level; high-level failures may knock it down. When to play safe and when to go all in is up to you.

No in-app purchases, and a session takes only a few minutes.

Highlights

  • Leaderboards: your five pieces add up to one total score, ranked on daily, weekly and per-slot national boards.
  • Weekly seasons: resources reset daily at 05:00; every Monday gear resets and the theme rotates between Xianxia, Western Knight, Western Mage and Wuxia sets.
  • Satisfying feedback: a retro gilded interface with charge-up beams, success flashes and shattering failures.

Game Theory Simulator: Mirror

Keep AI guessing.

  • In development
  • Sci-fi strategy & narrative
  • PC · Steam first
Steam page coming soon

You think you are changing your habits. Your opponent may be waiting for that change.

Game Theory Simulator: Mirror is a sci-fi strategy and narrative game in development. Enter a series of strategic tests as S-17 and, guided by Tutor, start from the rules, then face shifting constraints, time pressure and the question of what others intend.

Stick with your strategy or try something new? The game pairs prediction and counter-prediction with a story that unfolds as you play.

Meet S-17

Dark-blue test chambers, glassy interfaces and dot-matrix text set the look. Follow along for development updates and demo news.
