About the studio

Blood Moon Studio is a solo indie game studio. The aim is games with clear rules that are easy to start and still worth coming back to. The games so far fall into three groups:

  • Light casual gamesRules you understand at a glance and rounds that take a few minutes. Example: By Eye →
  • Simulator gamesBuilt around one complete system, where you make trade-offs with limited resources. Example: Forge Simulator →
  • Narrative indie gamesStrategy gameplay woven together with a story that unfolds as you play. Example: Game Theory Simulator: Mirror →

Every game supports English and Chinese and is out now or coming soon on Google Play, TapTap, itch.io, WeChat and Douyin mini games, Steam and the web.
