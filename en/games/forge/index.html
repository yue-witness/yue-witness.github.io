About the game

Forge Simulator is a small simulator game about upgrading gear. It is played in portrait mode, and a session takes only a few minutes.

Each day you receive a fixed number of enhancement stones to spend on five pieces of gear: weapon, helm, armor, gloves and boots. The goal is to take each one from +0 all the way to +10.

The success rate, cost and score of every level are shown in advance. A failed upgrade at low levels keeps your level; at high levels it may knock it down. With limited stones, every upgrade is a trade-off: push one piece as high as possible, or raise all five evenly?

There are no in-app purchases. Every player gets the same stones each day, so rankings come down to strategy and luck.

Highlights

  • Daily leaderboards: the scores of your five pieces add up to your total, ranked on daily, weekly and per-slot boards.
  • Weekly themes: stones reset every day at 05:00; every Monday your gear resets and the theme rotates between Xianxia, Western Knight, Western Mage and Wuxia.
  • Forging feedback: a gilded interface with charge-up beams, success flashes and gear that shatters when an upgrade fails.
