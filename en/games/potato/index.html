About the game

It's the middle of winter and you've taken a job peeling potatoes at a remote old processing plant. One workbench, one blunt knife and a mountain of potatoes in the yard. This batch's quota: one hundred thousand.

Drag the potato to peel it, drag the empty space to turn it. Strip the skin and dig out every eye: the cleaner the potato and the less you waste, the better the grade and the higher the price.

Save up for sharper knives, bigger baskets and carts, and spend skill points as you rise through the ranks. From a hand-cranked peeler to automatic peelers, conveyor belts and robotic grabbers, your single workbench slowly turns into a factory.

Warm your frozen hands, buy medicine when you fall ill, and keep the cat by the stove company. Now and then a note turns up inside a potato, and letters arrive from home. Your choices decide where the last cartload of potatoes goes.

Highlights

  • Peeling that feels real: potato shapes come from real scans; every stroke has depth and every eye has to go. Grade and waste set the price.
  • One finger, portrait mode: drag the potato to peel, drag the background to turn it, or learn Auto-Turn and just hold the knife.
  • Keep expanding: better knives, bigger baskets and carts, then peeling machines, conveyors and grabbers.
  • A story in the cold: notes, letters and two endings. No offline earnings and no energy meter: the plant runs as long as you keep peeling.
