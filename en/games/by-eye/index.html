About the game

We say “close enough” all the time: that steak is about half a pound, those two lines look the same length. Actually getting it right is harder than it sounds.

By Eye is a casual puzzle game about visual intuition. Each puzzle shows you a scene. There is nothing to calculate: take a look, turn the dial to set your estimate and submit. The game then reveals the real value and how far off you were.

Puzzles cover proportions, counts, motion, optical illusions and simple physics. The numbers change every time you retry, so memorizing answers won't help; only your eyes will.

Highlights

  • Six puzzle types: proportions, counts, moving objects, hands-on tasks, optical illusions, and math and physics.
  • Day and night: day levels let you take your time; at night the scene flashes once and you answer from memory.
  • Instant reveal: the real value and your error appear as soon as you submit.
  • Endless mode: unlocked after the day levels, with leaderboards.
