Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective November 14, 2026 · Developer: Yue Pan, independent developer (publishing as Blood Moon Studio)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Google Play version of Winter Potato Peeler Simulator ("the Game") handles information. The Game is a single-player offline game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own.

1. Information the developer collects

  • The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Your game progress, settings, language choice and in-game statistics are stored only on your device, in the Game's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes them permanently, and the developer cannot restore them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • Some in-game actions (for example, claiming certain equipment, learning skills, or waiving a rush-order penalty) offer an optional rewarded video. Ads are shown only after you choose to watch one. If no ad can be loaded, the game applies the action anyway.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google LLC). To deliver, measure and protect ads, the SDK may collect and process: the Android advertising ID and app set ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and operating system information, app information, ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. This information is processed by Google, not by the developer.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. In US states with applicable privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can change these choices at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown when it applies in your region).
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's Android settings.
  • Learn more: Google Privacy Policy https://policies.google.com/privacy ; How Google uses information from apps that use its services https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites .

3. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by the advertising SDK to load ads. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback in the game.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): used by the advertising SDK as described above.
  • The Game does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us.

5. Data security, retention and transfers

Because the developer does not collect personal information, there is nothing for the developer to retain or transfer. Information processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's policies and may be processed in countries other than your own.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Since the developer holds no personal information about you, requests concerning advertising data should be made through the controls described in Section 2 or directly to Google. You can stop all data processing by the Game at any time by uninstalling it.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the version number and effective date above and publish the new version in the Game and on our website.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact yue.witness@gmail.com.
